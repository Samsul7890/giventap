## Brand & Style

This design system blends **Tactile Soft Luxury** with **Micro-Glassmorphic Dreamscapes** to create an intimate, celebratory, and emotive keepsake experience. It anchors digital gifting into physical memory via NFC, making every touchpoint feel cherished, tactile, and warmly personal.

### Emotional Response
The interface should feel like opening a bespoke letterpress gift box wrapped in translucent vellum:
- **Intimate & Affectionate:** Warm, glowing, and welcoming; never cold, transactional, or purely utilitarian.
- **Serendipitous & Tactile:** Micro-interactions mimic the soft physical resistance and spring of real-world materials.
- **Celebratory:** Accents feel like confetti caught in morning light—delicate, uplifting, and luminous without being noisy.

### Style Directives
- **Layered Luminescence:** Surfaces use multi-stop pastel blurs and frosted translucent scrims rather than stark opaque planes.
- **Physical Micro-States:** Controls feature subtle dimensional highlights, gentle 0.985x active scale presses, and soft inner bevels to mimic real-world touch tags.
- **Sensory Refinement:** Crisp geometric sans anchors data and functional flows, while expressive high-contrast editorial serifs evoke sentiment during emotional reveals, notes, and milestones.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in glowing sunrise warmth: tender rose gold and romantic lavender supported by soft champagne amber, delicate peach, and refreshing mint.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D9777F` - Rose Gold Velvet):** Guides primary intent, love gestures, activation triggers, and heart actions.
- **Secondary (`#9B86BD` - Twilight Lavender):** Signifies digital NFC magic, mystery, audio memories, and deep emotional notes.
- **Tertiary (`#5DBA9D` - Fresh Mint Spark):** Communicates active tap-readiness, successfully saved keepsakes, and verified authentication.
- **Neutral (`#6C5B56` - Warm Cocoa Earth):** Supplies organic, high-readability text with soft warmth instead of cold synthetic carbon or grey.

### Supporting Chromatics & Tonal Scrims
- **Champagne Amber Glow (`#F5C58A`):** Used exclusively for celebratory bursts, milestone stars, and inner glass refractions.
- **Peach Foam (`#FFF5ED`):** Canvas foundation tint providing ambient warmth behind layered glass.
- **Vellum Surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`): Frosted translucent base for interactive cards and floating sheets.
- **Border Scrim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` top / `rgba(217, 119, 127, 0.18)` bottom): Creates a light-catching simulated chamfer on cards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear tension between intimate editorial craftsmanship and crisp, friendly utility.

### Expressive Editorial Accents (`Newsreader`)
- Use for human sentiment, heartfelt titles, NFC tap-in welcome messages, and gift letters.
- Use italicized styling selectively for emotional inflection words (e.g., *forever*, *yours*, *remember*).
- Optical sizing is applied automatically to maintain delicate serifs across display and body scales.

### Functional Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)
- Drives clarity and optimism for system navigation, status feedback, metadata, tag pairing guides, and action buttons.
- Rounded terminals mirror the organic, squircle-based structural tokens of the physical tag.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout prioritizing handheld gesture zones and thumb reach, while scaling elegantly onto desktop viewports via centered ergonomic glass canvases.

### Layout Model
- **Mobile Handheld (up to 640px):** Single-column stack with dynamic safe area padding (`margin: 1rem`). Interactive triggers anchor to a bottom floating dock within easy thumb reach.
- **Tablet / Phablet (641px - 1024px):** 6-column fluid structure with `1.25rem` gutters. Content containers max out at `680px` for conversational reading.
- **Desktop (1025px+):** 12-column layout centered within a maximum width of `1200px`. Keepsake stories use an offset 5/7 editorial split: interactive NFC tag preview pinned on the left, multimedia memory narrative scrolling on the right.

### Grid & Spacing Rhythm
- Built on a strict 4px/8px modular base. 
- Component internal padding strictly uses `space-md` (compact cards) and `space-lg` (memory storytelling cards).
- Macro section boundaries breathe with `space-2xl` to mimic curated luxury white space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows in favor of **Luminescent Ambient Diffusion** and **Micro-Glassmorphic Stratification**.

### Tiers of Depth
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Soft peach-toned ambient canvas (`#FFF8F3`) enriched with broad radial blur patches (`filter: blur(80px)`) blending soft rose, lavender, and champagne amber.
- **Level 1 (Frosted Keepsake Tiles):** 
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`
  - Shadow: `0 8px 32px -8px rgba(184, 134, 139, 0.16), 0 2px 8px rgba(184, 134, 139, 0.08)`
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays & Floating Sheets):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(24px) saturate(200%)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`
  - Shadow: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(155, 134, 189, 0.22), 0 6px 16px rgba(108, 91, 86, 0.06)`
- **Level 3 (Modal Portals & Physical NFC Simulators):**
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)`
  - Shadow: `0 32px 64px -16px rgba(108, 91, 86, 0.2), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8) inset`

### Lighting Direction
Simulate a single high-noon warm light source. Top borders receive bright frosted rim highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), while bottom edges soften into warm rose gold silhouettes.

## Shapes

The shape system is defined by the **Physical Squircle Tag** language. High corner curvature softens technological coldness and evokes physical resin or leather keepsakes.

### Roundedness Hierarchy
- **Pill (Fully Rounded / 9999px):** Applied to interactive buttons, status tags, celebratory counter chips, audio scrubber bars, and NFC scanning indicators.
- **Squircle Ultra (`rounded-3xl` / 1.75rem - 2rem):** Applied to keepsake memory containers, tap target cards, media viewports, and floating sheet envelopes.
- **Squircle Compact (`rounded-2xl` / 1rem - 1.25rem):** Applied to inner sub-cards, photo thumbnails, text input containers, and audio message clips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Keepsake Button:** 
  - Fill: Linear gradient from `#E88E95` to `#D9777F`.
  - Contrast Edge: Top inner highlight `1px inset rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
  - Shadow: `0 10px 24px -6px rgba(217, 119, 127, 0.45)`.
  - Text: `#FFFFFF`, `label-lg`, font weight 600.
  - Interaction: Hover raises elevation (`translateY(-1px)`); Active state compresses physically (`scale(0.98)`, shadow collapses to `0 4px 12px rgba(217, 119, 127, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Glass:** 
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.5)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.
  - Text: `#6C5B56`.
  - Hover: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Celebration Badges:** 
  - Pill shape, height `28px`, padding `0 12px`.
  - NFC Active: Background `rgba(93, 186, 157, 0.15)`, text `#3D8F75`, border `1px solid rgba(93, 186, 157, 0.3)`.
  - Gift Tag Moment: Background `rgba(245, 197, 138, 0.25)`, text `#8E5B19`, accompanied by a micro sparkle icon.

### Cards (Squircle Keepsakes)
- Border radius: `28px` (`rounded-3xl`).
- Padding: `24px` mobile, `32px` desktop.
- Surface: Glassmorphic micro-scrim (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`), 20px blur, double-layered warm drop shadow.
- Inner elements stay bounded by 16px internal padding margins to preserve the squircle curvature.

### Input Fields
- Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
- Border: `1.5px solid rgba(217, 119, 127, 0.2)`.
- Border Radius: `16px`.
- Padding: `14px 18px`.
- Focus State: Border switches to `#D9777F` with a soft halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px rgba(217, 119, 127, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Toggle Controls
- Custom rounded circles (`20px`).
- Inactive: Frosted translucent disc with warm cocoa border.
- Active: Solid Rose Gold `#D9777F` fill displaying a clean, hand-crafted white check icon with spring-like entrance animation.

### Specialized Platform Components
- **NFC Tap Pulse Target:** Concentric expanding rings featuring multi-stop gradient borders (`#D9777F` to `#9B86BD`) that pulse gently to guide phone proximity.
- **Audio Voice-Note Capsule:** Squircle pill containing a waveform rendered in `#9B86BD` over peach frosted glass, paired with a tactile play trigger.
- **Confetti & Sparkle Shimmer Scrim:** Ambient canvas overlay using lightweight CSS particle bursts for first-time tag unlock moments.
